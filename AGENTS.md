# Nexus Lab Agent Instructions

- Follow `../nexus/AGENTS.md` and `../nexus/.ai/standards/pascal.md`.
- Organize examples under `examples/` by the Nexus capability they demonstrate.
- Keep runtime implementations and production build templates in their owning repositories.
- Keep test-only fixtures with their owning tests, rather than moving them here.
- Use NexusFPC, nxbuild and Forge. Do not introduce Lazarus projects or tooling.
- Preserve imported licenses, notices, author metadata and source headers.
- Maintain provenance in `../nexus/ATTRIBUTIONS.md`; link to that record here.
- Put generated build output under `output/`.
